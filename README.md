### Featured Growth Case Study

**[Wispr Flow India: Growth & Monetization Diagnostic](https://github.com/rahuljtom/wispr-flow-india-growth-study)**  
**[Looker Studio Dashboard ↗](https://lookerstudio.google.com/reporting/bdcc93a1-66f1-4f6b-9496-c69d8ba01035)** · **[10-Slide Deck ↗](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1itgf-Vjq8WL_pxCx-8aFIfCaeLe-iqgf/edit?usp=sharing)** · **[Repository & Ledger ↗](https://github.com/rahuljtom/wispr-flow-india-growth-study)**

India is reported as **~14% of Wispr Flow's installs** but **~2% of in-app-purchase revenue** (`0.143×` the global revenue per install; Sensor Tower via TechCrunch). Wispr says India is its second-largest base by users and paying subscribers, so some India revenue may be billed outside the app stores (a hypothesis). Built from public data only, with every figure traced to a numbers ledger, to unpack the gap and size 3 India tests:

- **Pricing:** the India list price (`₹320/mo` billed annually) is `0.283` of the global price, leaving a `0.504` residual (`0.22–1.22` across 12 scenarios). It is an arithmetic decomposition, not a cause.
- **Review signals (`micro-F1 = 0.784`, `κ = 0.830` on a held-out gold split):** a PII-scrubbed Gemini tagging workflow on an interim sample of 321 Play reviews. Pricing and paywall themes appear in **26.9% of 1–2★ reviews vs 11.2% overall (2.4×)**; onboarding/permission-setup themes in **13.5% vs 4.4% (about 3×, intervals overlap)**. Directional: recent, not India-specific.
- **Competition & experiments:** scored 10 rivals on 5 criteria (Gboard leads on reach and price relevance; Superflow is the closest dedicated rival on Hinglish and Accessibility overlap), then power-sized 3 proposed India experiments (`1,376` to `3,788` users per arm). None has been run.
